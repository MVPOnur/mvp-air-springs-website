      Inside and out

      What goes into an MVP air spring

      Every MVP air spring has its part number molded into the rubber. When it is time to replace one, read the number off the old air spring and look it up here.

      1. Part number molded into the rubberNo sticker to peel off.
      2. 4.5–5.0 mm thick rubberThe wall of the rubber bellows.
      3. 4140 alloy steel studs4140 is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel.
      4. Leak-tested after final assemblyEvery air spring is checked for leaks before it leaves the factory.
      5. 3-year warrantyAcross the whole MVP range.
